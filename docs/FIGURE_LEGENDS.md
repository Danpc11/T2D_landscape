# Figure legends

Panels carry no titles; everything is described here. Statistics: permutation tests with the null
distributions shown; FDR by Benjamini–Hochberg over the gene set indicated; error bars defined per panel.
Coherence is always the leave-one-out statistic (mean cosine of each individual's displacement to the
group mean computed without that individual).

---

**Fig. 1 | The metabolic organs share one transcriptional state, each person occupies one position in it, and that state drifts continuously.**
**a**, The cellular sheaf used throughout: each organ's coexpression network contributes a stalk (the spectral subspace of its Laplacian), edges carry orthogonal alignments, and the sheaf energy *E* is the sum of Grassmann distances between aligned stalks, zero when the organs share one architecture. **b**, Sheaf energy by glycaemic stage in the discovery cohorts (islet GSE76895, muscle GSE18732, adipose GSE27951; squares, mean ± 95% CI of the gene-correspondence null, *B* = 200; circles, observed). z = −10.6, −7.5 and −7.0 for healthy, intermediate and T2D. **c**, Same statistic in GTEx v11 donors with muscle, subcutaneous adipose tissue and pancreas from the same individual (*n* = 253); grey, null distribution; blue line, observed (z = −26). **d**, Canonical correlation between a donor's position in one organ and in another (circles) against the distribution obtained when donors are shuffled between organs (violins, 400 draws); the fourth pair is subcutaneous versus omental adipose tissue in 19 living surgical patients (GSE20950). *P* ≤ 0.002 for all four. **e**, Effect of adjusting for technical covariates in GTEx: |z| of the sheaf energy (blue, left axis) and muscle–adipose canonical ρ (orange, right axis) before and after regressing RNA integrity, ischaemic time and sequencing batch in addition to age, sex and Hardy scale. **f**, Cross-validated R² of whole-blood expression predicting the same donor's organ state (5-fold, 10 blood components → 5 organ components, *n* = 224). **g**, Number of basins above the bootstrap stability threshold τ in each cohort, at three kernel bandwidths; no cohort retains two basins across bandwidths. **h**, Covariate-adjusted embedding of 77 islet donors (GSE50244) coloured by HbA1c stratum, illustrating continuous drift; Cramér's V between mixture component and stage = 0.17. **i**, The genes that carry the shared individual position: Pearson r of each gene's residual expression with the canonical variate shared by muscle and adipose tissue, for the 28 genes with the largest effect of consistent sign in all three organs. These are the drivers identified by the present approach, to be compared with the canonical differential-expression drivers in Fig. 2g,h.

**Fig. 2 | The disease is not visible at rest in any organ; it is visible in the response, and muscle is where that response fragments.**
**a**, Cohorts analysed per organ, at rest and with biopsies taken before and during a hyperinsulinaemic clamp; ranges are *n* per stage or per group. **b**, Sheaf energy between the three organs by stage (as in Fig. 1b): coherence weakens with stage, but no stage-to-stage difference reaches significance (*P* = 0.15–0.53, permutation). **c**, Critical-transition index I_c by stage, one line per cohort, coloured by organ: no stage is critical in any organ. **d**, Ratio of state dispersion in T2D to healthy with 95% bootstrap CI at equal *n*, one point per cohort; all intervals cross 1. **e**, Under insulin the organs differ: filled squares, ratio of response magnitude (resistant / sensitive); open circles, 1 + Δcoherence (resistant − sensitive). Adipose tissue loses magnitude and keeps direction; muscle keeps magnitude and loses direction. **f**, Summary of what each organ contributed; muscle is the only organ with paired insulin biopsies in all three states. **g**, The canonical read-out for comparison: genes differentially expressed at rest (T2D versus control, or obese versus normal weight in METSIM) at FDR < 0.1 in each cohort, after adjusting for the covariates of each study. Islet and adipose tissue yield hundreds to thousands of genes; muscle yields two. **h**, Set enrichment of the same contrasts: z of the mean |t| of each curated set against 2,000 random sets of equal size; asterisks mark P < 0.05. The canonical analysis recovers the expected signatures (inflammation and complement, lipid and adipogenesis, ECM) in islet and adipose tissue, and almost nothing in muscle; none of these sets concerns the response to insulin, which is where the signal in muscle lies (Figs. 4 and 5).

**Fig. 3 | The resting muscle transcriptome carries no information about insulin sensitivity.**
**a**, Design (GSE182120): resting vastus lateralis biopsies from 49 individuals profiled in two laboratories, all with insulin sensitivity measured by hyperinsulinaemic–euglycaemic clamp; expression was residualised on laboratory, age and BMI before correlating with the M-value. **b**, Quantile–quantile plot of the genome-wide association with the M-value (21,595 genes); no gene reaches FDR < 0.1 and 14 reach *P* < 0.01 where ~215 are expected by chance; known markers in blue. **c**, Volcano plot of T2D versus NGT at rest; no gene at FDR < 0.1. **d**, Mean |ρ| with the M-value for curated gene sets (sizes in parentheses) against random sets of equal size (grey bars, 95% of 2,000 draws); only the oxidative programme separates from the null. **e**, Per-gene ρ with the M-value for the clock-output genes examined in Figs. 4 and 5; all *P* > 0.2.

**Fig. 4 | The healthy response to insulin is coordinated across individuals, assembled over hours, and includes a reset of the clock output.**
**a**, Definition of the measure: each person's displacement is the difference between the insulin and basal biopsy in the principal subspace of the 800 most variable genes; coherence is the mean cosine of each displacement to the leave-one-out group mean. **b**, Displacements of 20 insulin-sensitive individuals (GSE22309) projected onto the group mean direction and its principal orthogonal direction; coherence 0.77. **c**, Coherence against time after the stimulus in four independent healthy datasets (*n* per point indicated). **d**, Paired *t* per gene at 30 min versus 4 h in 12 healthy men (GSE9105); the 55 genes of the replicated healthy programme in blue. **e**, Gene sets in the healthy 4-h response (GSE22309, insulin-sensitive group) against random sets of equal size (grey, 95%). **f**, Paired *t* for clock-output genes at 30 min and 4 h (GSE9105) and at 4 h in an independent cohort (GSE22309). **g**, Control for the repeat-biopsy artefact: mean paired *t* of immediate-early genes and of clock-output genes at each time point; immediate-early genes respond at every time, the clock output only at 4 h.

**Fig. 5 | Insulin resistance preserves the magnitude of the response but fragments its direction and uncouples the clock.**
**a**, Magnitude of each individual's displacement by group (log scale; bars, mean ± s.d.). **b**, Loss of coherence relative to the insulin-sensitive group (circles) against the distribution obtained by permuting group labels (violins, 2,000 permutations); *P* as indicated. **c**, The same comparison restricted to individuals whose two biopsies were hybridised in the same batch (filled) alongside all pairs (open); *n* per group indicated. **d**, Direction of each person's response relative to the healthy mean direction (north), as a rose of angles; healthy individuals cluster at north, insulin-resistant and diabetic individuals spread around the circle. Group cosine between the insulin-sensitive and T2D mean directions 0.20 against a null of 0.85 (*P* < 0.001). **e**, Coherence across all muscle datasets analysed, including two arms of an independent prediabetes trial (GSE157988). **f**, |Response to insulin| per clock-output gene by group; dark sectors, repressed; light sectors, induced. Asterisks, FDR < 0.05 for the group × insulin interaction versus the insulin-sensitive group (permutation, BH over nine clock genes); daggers, FDR < 0.1 for T2D. **g**, Effect of chronic insulin on the same genes in primary myotubes from donors with normal glucose tolerance and with T2D (GSE182117). **h**, Amplitude of the 24-h oscillation (cosinor fit) of clock-output genes in the same myotubes; mean ± s.d. **i**, Fate of the 55-gene healthy programme in insulin-resistant and diabetic muscle: kept (same sign, |t| > 2), lost (|t| < 2) or inverted.

**Fig. 6 | Adipose tissue fails by magnitude, and interventions restore magnitude without restoring coordination.**
**a**, Magnitude (grey, left axis) and coherence (blue, right axis) of the adipose response to a clamp in non-obese individuals, in obese individuals, and in the same 23 women two years after bariatric surgery (Rydén et al. 2016). **b**, The same quantities in an independent adipose clamp cohort (GSE26637). **c**, Magnitude ratio (resistant / sensitive) in the two adipose cohorts and in muscle, on one scale. **d**, Coherence before and after 10 weeks of nicotinamide mononucleotide or placebo in prediabetic women (GSE157988); *P* from a paired permutation test that swaps the two time points within each participant. **e**, Change in coherence detectable with 80% power as a function of group size, assuming s.d. = 0.3; dashed lines mark the two cohorts available. **f**, Summary: healthy organs respond along one direction; insulin-resistant muscle responds in scattered directions, insulin-resistant adipose tissue responds in the same direction with half the magnitude.
